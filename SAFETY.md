# Safety · Segurança

**This is not a certified medical device** (not registered with ANVISA). It is provided as-is, with no warranty. Build and use it at your own risk.

- Get the fit checked by a health professional.
- Stop using it if you feel pain or numbness, or see redness or sores.
- Check parts for cracks before each use. Don't use it where a failure could cause injury.
- Making or selling this device for other people may require health-authority approval (in Brazil, ANVISA).
